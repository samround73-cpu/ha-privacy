Privacy Policy

This application is a personal, private integration that connects the owner's own Home Assistant instance to the owner's own Google Nest devices. It is used only by its owner.

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data with any third party. Data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to display and control the owner's own devices within Home Assistant.
